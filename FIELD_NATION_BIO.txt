KnC Automations LLC is a woman-owned, veteran-led technology infrastructure company supporting structured cabling, fiber optics, network infrastructure, physical security, IT support, and automation projects for commercial and government-facing clients.

Core capabilities:
- Structured cabling: CAT5e, CAT6, CAT6A, patch panels, racks, labeling and testing
- Fiber optics: backbone installation, terminations, splicing coordination and OTDR-focused closeout support
- Network infrastructure: switch refresh support, wireless access points, VoIP and troubleshooting
- Physical security: CCTV and access control infrastructure support
- Automation: custom workflows, dashboards, CRM improvements and AI-enabled business tools

Why KnC:
- Woman-owned and veteran-led
- Responsive project communication
- Clear scopes, documented work and practical recommendations
- Commercial and government-facing project support
