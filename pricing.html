What affects price

The cleanest quote comes from a clear scope.

For cabling, fiber, CCTV, and access control, pricing depends on labor hours, pathway complexity, lift requirements, cable type, materials, testing, closeout documentation, wage requirements, and access restrictions.

  • Number and type of drops, cameras, doors, devices, or racks
  • Ceiling type, pathway, conduit, lift, or access requirements
  • Testing, labeling, as-built, and closeout documentation needs
  • After-hours, badge, security, insurance, or prevailing wage requirements

Best information to send

Drawings, photos of existing closets, site address, requested timeline, known device counts, and any required standards or government clauses.
