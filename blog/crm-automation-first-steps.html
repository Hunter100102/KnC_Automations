What to automate first in your CRM

A CRM should make the business easier to run. If your team is still manually updating everything, the CRM is probably underused.

Start with the basics

  • Lead intake from website forms
  • Automatic follow-up reminders
  • Simple customer status dashboards
  • Required field checks to reduce missing data
  • Notifications when a deal or project needs attention

Avoid overbuilding

The best CRM improvements usually start small. Build the workflow that saves time now, test it, then improve from there.

When to ask for help

If your CRM has become a place where information goes to get lost, it may be time to map the workflow and automate the most repetitive pieces.
