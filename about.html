About KnC

Woman-owned, veteran-led, and built around practical execution.

KnC Automations LLC is an Atlanta-area technology infrastructure company focused on helping organizations build secure, connected and efficient operations.

Our mission

We help businesses, agencies and prime contractors solve infrastructure and technology problems with clear scopes, disciplined execution and responsive communication.

KnC’s service model blends low-voltage infrastructure, IT project experience and software automation capability, giving customers a flexible partner for both field and operational technology needs.

Brand position

Leadership approach

Professional, accountable and transparent.

We aim to communicate like a larger contractor while staying hands-on enough to care about the details: access requirements, materials, labeling, punch lists, safety, testing and closeout documentation.

What we value

  • Clear documentation and expectations
  • Responsive customer communication
  • Practical recommendations over unnecessary complexity
